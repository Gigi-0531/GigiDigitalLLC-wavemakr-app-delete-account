Delete Your Ripple Account

If you would like to delete your Ripple account and associated personal data, you may request deletion at any time.

Important: Deleting your account is permanent and cannot be undone.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Email us at gigy.3663@gmail.com
  2. Use the subject line: Delete My Ripple Account
  3. Include the email address associated with your Ripple account

Example email subject: Delete My Ripple Account

Example email body:
Hi, I would like to request deletion of my Ripple account associated with this email address.

What Will Be Deleted

When your deletion request is processed, we may delete:

  • Your account profile information
  • Your login/account credentials
  • Your user-submitted content, where applicable
  • Associated personal data stored in our systems

What May Be Retained

We may retain limited information if required for:

  • Legal compliance
  • Fraud prevention or abuse monitoring
  • Security and recordkeeping purposes

Processing Time

We aim to process account deletion requests within a reasonable timeframe after verifying the request.
